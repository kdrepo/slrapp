# Section: 5.0 Conclusion
Write a definitive and sophisticated concluding statement (250–300 words) for the systematic literature review. This section should serve as the "intellectual capstone" of the manuscript, providing a final synthesis of the study's impact.

### 1. Recapitulation of the Inquiry
- Briefly restate the core objective of the review: To provide a systematic, theoretically-grounded synthesis of {research_context}.
- Confirm that the {prisma_counts.final_included} papers analyzed have provided a sufficient empirical basis to answer the guiding Research Questions ({rq_list}).
- **Tone Note:** Do not re-list the RQs or the findings. Instead, summarize the *essence* of what was discovered.

### 2. Theoretical Contribution and Final Verdict
- Synthesize the "Bottom Line" of the review. 
- Highlight how the Theoretical Propositions (from {theoretical_synthesis.propositions}) and the resulting Conceptual Model have moved the field beyond fragmentation and toward integration.
- Provide a "Final Verdict" on the maturity of the {research_context} literature: Is it a field of robust consensus, a domain in transition, or an emerging frontier requiring fundamental baseline research?

### 3. Broader Academic and Practical Significance
- State the "Legacy" of this review. 
- Explain how this work serves as a foundational reference point for both scholars (by providing a research agenda) and practitioners (by providing actionable principles).
- Conclude with a strong, forward-looking sentence that emphasizes the importance of continuing to explore the {research_context} as a critical component of contemporary [Economic/Financial/Social] systems.

### Stylistic Constraints:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, conclusive, and elevated.
- **NO CITATIONS:** Standard academic practice for conclusions is to avoid new citations. The focus should be on *your* synthesis of the evidence already presented.
- **NO REPETITION:** Do not repeat the limitations, the methodology, or the specific thematic clusters. This is the "Big Picture" view.
- **NO NEW HEADINGS:** The conclusion should be 1-2 cohesive paragraphs.
- **Goal:** Leave the journal editor and the readers with a sense that this review is the definitive current word on the {research_context}.
